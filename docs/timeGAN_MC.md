# TimeGAN for Monte Carlo Simulation

TimeGAN is a generative adversarial network architecture specifically designed for time-series data, combining adversarial learning with supervised sequence modeling to preserve both temporal dynamics and marginal distributions (Yoon et al., 2019).

In this research context, TimeGAN can be used as an alternative Monte Carlo engine to generate synthetic price or return paths that better reflect empirical properties of financial time series, such as autocorrelation, volatility clustering, and non-linear dependencies, compared to traditional parametric models.

Synthetic paths generated by TimeGAN may be used to:
- stress-test trading strategies under realistic but unseen scenarios,
- estimate the distribution of performance metrics (e.g. returns, drawdowns),
- complement historical backtests by expanding the effective sample size.

Care must be taken to avoid information leakage during training and to validate that generated series are statistically consistent with real data before downstream use.

## Reference
- Yoon, J., Jarrett, D., & van der Schaar, M. (2019). *Time-series Generative Adversarial Networks*. NeurIPS.
